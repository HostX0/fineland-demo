# Fineland product overview

## A more connected assessment workflow

Fineland is a clinic-style demonstration for organizing an adaptive-behavior interview from intake through report preparation. It brings together steps that are often handled across paper forms, scoring references, calculators, and separate report documents.

The experience is designed to help clinicians see how a more connected workflow could reduce manual scoring and transcription effort, make follow-up comparisons easier to review, and give more time back to direct work with children and families.

## The challenge

The requesting clinician described spending 45–60 minutes or more on manual scoring, table lookup, checking routing rules, and assembling reports. Repeating those steps across follow-up visits added administrative work while children and families waited for assessment and the next step in care.

The clinician estimated about 10 minutes or less after using the system. These are client-reported estimates, not an independently measured outcome or guarantee. The benefit is less routine administrative work and more time available for clinical attention; time savings vary by case and clinic process.

## What the demo includes

### Case intake

Create fictional cases, record interview details, and calculate chronological age from dates. Choose the reference interview route or a separate clinic age-cap workflow.

### Interview and scoring

Enter responses using 0, 1, or 2, or use raw values where the workflow calls for them. The experience demonstrates routing, basal and ceiling behavior, domain scoring, v-scales, and the Adaptive Behavior Composite (ABC). The ABC excludes Motor.

### Results and follow-up

Review domain and composite results, equivalent age, and Growth Scale Value (GSV). Compare follow-up assessments, explore strengths and pairwise comparisons, and view maladaptive behavior and critical items in separate areas.

### Case and report workflow

Search or archive cases, prepare a report, and print or save it as PDF through the browser. Clinic settings let you add clinic identity and a logo for the report experience.

## Who it is for

The demo is intended for clinicians, clinic teams, and decision-makers evaluating how a digital workflow might fit their assessment and reporting process. It uses fictional records and demonstration item wording so visitors can explore the interaction without entering patient information.

## Demonstration status

Fineland is a frontend-only demonstration with browser-local storage and no sign-in. It is not a production clinic record system, and it must not be used with real patient information or for clinical decisions. Exact implementation of scoring behavior does not establish clinical validation or replace authorized use of the instrument.

Vineland-3 is published by Pearson. Fineland is an independent product demonstration and is not endorsed by Pearson. See [Assessment and scoring notes](ASSESSMENT.md) and [Privacy and demo data](PRIVACY.md).
